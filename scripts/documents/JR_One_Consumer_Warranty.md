::lead
JR One Aluminum LLC warrants the original purchaser of all aluminum gutters and related exterior products installed by JR One Aluminum as follows:

::section Coverage

::item A
Twenty (20) year manufacturer paint warranty on painted aluminum products. Painted aluminum will be free of paint defects including cracking, peeling, and chipping for the warranty period.

::item B
Three (3) year workmanship warranty on any products installed by JR One Aluminum. Installed products will be free of workmanship defects and will maintain proper function during the warranty period.

::section Conditions

::body
This warranty shall remain in effect only if the gutter system has not been subject to conditions beyond JR One Aluminum's control, including but not limited to accidental damage, extreme weather conditions, acts of nature, improper use of caustic chemicals, foundation settling, third-party alterations, and failure to perform routine maintenance.

::callout
How to make a warranty claim:
--
Call us at (844) 444-3114 or email info@jronegutters.com. Describe the issue and we will schedule an inspection. If the issue falls within warranty coverage, we schedule the repair at no charge. We do not make warranty claims difficult — we would rather fix a problem fast than argue about it.

::note
This warranty is effective from the date of substantial completion of the installation.
