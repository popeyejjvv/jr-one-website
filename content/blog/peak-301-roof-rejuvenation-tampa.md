## How Peak 301 Works (The Actual Science)

Asphalt shingles are made with petroleum-based oils and resins that keep the material flexible and weather-resistant. Over time, typically 8 to 15 years in Florida's intense UV environment, those oils evaporate. The shingles dry out, become brittle, and start failing: curling at the edges, losing granules, cracking under thermal cycling.

Peak 301 is a soy-based methyl ester (a bio-oil) that's sprayed onto the roof surface. The molecules are sized to penetrate into the shingle material and replace the lost petroleum oils, restoring flexibility and weather resistance.

It's not a coating that sits on top. It's not a sealant. It's a material-level restoration of the shingle's original chemistry.

The product is third-party tested under ASTM D6944, the industry standard cyclic flexibility test for asphalt shingles. Treated shingles show flexibility comparable to new shingles after the same number of accelerated weathering cycles.

## What Peak 301 Does

- **Restores shingle flexibility:** reduces curling and cracking
- **Slows granule loss:** the protective surface layer stays intact longer
- **Extends roof life by 5 to 15 years:** depending on current condition and remaining useful life
- **Improves wind resistance:** flexible shingles hold up better in storms than brittle ones
- **Reduces thermal stress damage:** brittle shingles crack with daily expansion/contraction; flexible shingles flex

## What Peak 301 Does NOT Do

- **Fix structural damage:** rotten decking, damaged underlayment, or missing shingles need actual repair
- **Work on non-shingle roofs:** tile, metal, flat/membrane, and slate roofs are not candidates
- **Replace a roof that's already failed:** if shingles are severely deteriorated, missing, or leaking, the roof needs replacement, not rejuvenation
- **Prevent all future issues:** it extends life, it doesn't make the roof immortal
- **Restore appearance permanently:** the slight darkening from treatment fades within 30 days back to roof's natural color
- **Replace ongoing maintenance:** keeping gutters clean, trimming overhanging branches, and addressing storm damage promptly still matters

## The Tampa Factor: Why Florida Shingles Age Faster

Florida's UV intensity is roughly 30 to 40 percent higher than the national average. Combined with year-round heat and high humidity, the effective aging rate for asphalt shingles in Tampa is 1.3 to 1.5 times what it is in northern states.

A roof rated for 25 years in Ohio may show meaningful aging at 15 to 18 years in Tampa. A 30-year architectural shingle in Tampa often performs like a 22-year shingle in Pennsylvania.

Peak 301 is especially relevant in Florida because the gap between "roof is aging" and "roof needs replacement" is compressed by our climate. Catching it in that window, after aging starts but before failure, is where Peak 301 delivers the most value. Wait too long and the product can't recover the shingle. Apply too early and you're treating a roof that didn't need it yet.

The sweet spot in Tampa is typically year 8 to 15 for architectural shingles, year 7 to 12 for 3-tab.
