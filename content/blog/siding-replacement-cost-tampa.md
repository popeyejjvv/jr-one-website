# Siding Replacement Cost in Tampa: Materials, Pricing, and What to Expect

If your home's siding is faded, cracked, warped, or rotting, replacement is often more cost-effective than patching. Tampa's climate accelerates siding deterioration — UV, humidity, rain, and storm impacts all take their toll.

Here's what replacement costs and how to choose the right material for Florida.

## Cost by Material

| Material | Cost Per Sq Ft (Installed) | Typical Home Cost (1,750 sq ft) | Lifespan in FL |
|----------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|----------------|
| Vinyl | $3 - $7 | $5,250 - $12,250 | 15-25 years |
| Aluminum | $5 - $9 | $8,750 - $15,750 | 30-40 years |
| Fiber Cement (HardiePlank) | $8 - $12 | $14,000 - $21,000 | 30-50 years |
| Engineered Wood | $6 - $10 | $10,500 - $17,500 | 20-30 years |
| Natural Wood | $7 - $12 | $12,250 - $21,000 | 10-20 years (FL) |

## Material Comparison for Florida

### Vinyl Siding

**Pros:** Cheapest option, decent color variety, low maintenance, doesn't rot.

**Cons for Florida:** Warps in extreme heat (south and west facing walls regularly exceed 140F), becomes brittle and cracks after UV degradation, fades and chalks within 5-8 years, expands/contracts creating gaps.

**Verdict:** Budget option. Acceptable for covered or shaded walls. Not recommended for full sun exposure in Florida.

### Aluminum Siding

**Pros:** Doesn't warp in heat, UV stable, won't rot or harbor mold, wind resistant, dent-repairable, fire resistant, recyclable.

**Cons:** Can dent from impact (repairable), slightly higher cost than vinyl, fewer decorative profiles than fiber cement.

**Verdict:** Best all-around choice for Florida. Handles every climate challenge without degradation.

### Fiber Cement (HardiePlank)

**Pros:** Looks like painted wood, extremely durable, fire resistant, termite proof, holds paint well, premium appearance.

**Cons:** Heaviest material (requires stronger framing), highest installed cost, requires repainting every 10-15 years, absorbs moisture if paint fails.

**Verdict:** Premium choice for homeowners who want the wood look without the wood problems. Best ROI on upscale homes.

## What Affects the Price

- **Home size and stories.** More square footage and height = more cost. Two-story work requires scaffolding.
- **Old siding removal.** Stripping existing siding adds $1-$3 per sq ft. Some installs go over existing siding to save on removal.
- **Trim and corner work.** J-channel, corner posts, window trim, and soffit transitions add detail labor.
- **Insulation.** Insulated siding or adding house wrap during re-siding adds cost but improves energy efficiency.
- **Repairs underneath.** If removing old siding reveals rot, moisture damage, or termite damage in the sheathing, that must be repaired first.

## Signs You Need Siding Replacement

- Widespread fading, chalking, or color loss
- Cracks, holes, or warped panels
- Rising energy bills (failed insulation behind deteriorated siding)
- Mold or mildew that returns after cleaning
- Soft spots or rot in wood siding
- Multiple patch repairs that make the wall look inconsistent
- Storm damage across large sections

## The Bottom Line

Siding replacement in Tampa is a significant investment, but the right material choice turns it into a 30-40 year solution. Aluminum and fiber cement both handle Florida's climate. Vinyl is the budget path but has real limitations in our heat and UV environment.
