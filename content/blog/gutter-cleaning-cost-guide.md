# How Much Does Gutter Cleaning Cost? (2026 Pricing Guide)

Gutter cleaning is one of those services where pricing is all over the map. You might get a quote for $99 from one company and $450 from another for the same house. Here's how to know what's fair, what's cheap (and why), and what you should actually expect to pay.

## Average Gutter Cleaning Costs

### National Averages

| Home Type | Price Range | Average |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| Single-story, small (under 1,500 sq ft) | $75 - $150 | $100 |
| Single-story, standard (1,500-2,500 sq ft) | $100 - $200 | $150 |
| Two-story home | $150 - $350 | $225 |
| Multi-story or complex roofline | $250 - $500 | $350 |

### Tampa Bay Pricing

Tampa runs slightly higher than the national average because of heavier debris loads (year-round shedding from live oaks and pines) and more frequent need (2-4x per year vs. 1-2x in northern climates).

| Home Type | Tampa Price Range |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Single-story ranch | $150 - $250 |
| Two-story standard | $200 - $350 |
| Two-story with heavy tree coverage | $250 - $400 |
| Large or complex homes | $350 - $500+ |

## What Affects the Price

**Linear footage of gutters.** More gutter = more time = higher price. A 150-foot gutter system costs less to clean than a 300-foot system. Most companies price by the job (based on a visual estimate of your home), but the underlying math is per-foot.

**Number of stories.** Every additional story increases the height, ladder work, and safety requirements. Two-story cleaning is always more than single-story.

**Debris level.** Gutters packed with decomposed leaves and shingle sludge take longer than gutters with just a light leaf layer. If you haven't cleaned in 2+ years, expect to pay toward the high end.

**Downspout clogs.** Basic cleaning includes debris removal from the gutter channel. Snaking or flushing clogged downspouts is sometimes an add-on charge ($25-$50 per downspout) but should be included in any professional service.

**Roof access difficulty.** Steep pitches, fragile tile roofs, limited ladder placement areas, or surrounding obstacles (fences, pools, landscaping) add time and difficulty.

**Add-on services.** Some companies offer roof debris clearing, minor repairs (re-securing hangers, sealing small leaks), or gutter brightening (exterior cleaning) as add-ons.

## What Should Be Included in a Professional Cleaning

A proper gutter cleaning — not a quick scoop-and-go — includes:

1. **Full debris removal** from all gutter channels (scooped, not just blown out)
2. **Downspout flushing** to confirm water flows freely to grade
3. **Flow test** — water run through every section to verify drainage
4. **Debris hauled away** — not dumped on your landscaping or left on the roof
5. **Basic inspection** — loose hangers, leaking seams, fascia condition noted and reported

If someone quotes you $75 and plans to spend 20 minutes with a leaf blower, you're getting a blowout, not a cleaning. The debris ends up on your roof, in your landscaping, and eventually back in the gutters.

## How Often Do Florida Gutters Need Cleaning?

| Tree Coverage | Recommended Frequency |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Heavy (oak, pine, palm) | Every 3 months (4x/year) |
| Moderate (mixed trees) | Every 4-6 months (2-3x/year) |
| Light (minimal trees) | Every 6-12 months (1-2x/year) |
| Gutter guards installed | Once per year (surface clearing) |

Florida's trees don't follow a single "leaf season" the way northern states do. Live oaks shed in spring. Pines drop needles year-round. Palm fronds fall after storms. There's always something going into your gutters.

## Annual Cleaning Costs vs. Gutter Guard Investment

| Approach | Year 1 Cost | 5-Year Cost | 10-Year Cost |
|----------|------------|------------|-------------|
| Quarterly cleaning ($200 avg) | $800 | $4,000 | $8,000 |
| Semi-annual cleaning ($200 avg) | $400 | $2,000 | $4,000 |
| Gutter guards + annual clearing | $1,500-$3,000 | $2,000-$3,500 | $2,500-$4,000 |

For homes with heavy tree coverage requiring quarterly cleaning, gutter guards pay for themselves within 3-4 years and save thousands over the decade.

## Red Flags in Gutter Cleaning Quotes

- **Price seems too low** ($50-$75 for a full home) — they're either cutting corners or planning upsells on-site
- **No downspout flushing included** — half the job
- **"Blow out" instead of hand cleaning** — debris goes on your roof and yard, not off your property
- **Pressure to buy guards or repairs on the spot** — inspect-and-upsell is a common tactic. Legit companies separate the cleaning from the sales pitch
- **No insurance** — if someone falls off a ladder on your property without workers' comp, that's your liability
