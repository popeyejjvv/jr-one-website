# Fascia Rot in Florida: Causes, Prevention, and Permanent Repair

Fascia rot is the most common hidden damage on Tampa Bay homes. It starts behind the gutter where nobody looks, progresses for months or years without visible symptoms, and by the time you see peeling paint or soft spots, the wood is already compromised.

Here's how it happens, how to catch it early, and how to stop it permanently.

## What Causes Fascia Rot

### Water Behind the Gutter

The #1 cause. When gutters leak at seams, overflow, or pull away from the fascia, water gets trapped between the gutter back and the fascia board. In Tampa's humidity, that moisture never fully evaporates. The wood stays damp, and fungal rot sets in.

You can't see this damage from the ground. It's hidden behind the gutter. Many homeowners don't discover it until new gutters are installed and the old ones come down.

### Humidity Saturation

Tampa averages 74% relative humidity year-round. Wood fascia absorbs atmospheric moisture constantly. Even without a gutter leak, unpainted or poorly painted fascia absorbs enough ambient moisture to begin deteriorating over 10-15 years.

### UV Paint Breakdown

Florida sun destroys paint faster than any other climate factor. When the paint film breaks down, it loses its seal against moisture. Bare or flaking paint on fascia is the beginning of the rot cycle - water gets in, humidity keeps it there, and the wood softens from the inside out.

## How to Spot Fascia Rot Early

**From the ground (no ladder):**
- Paint peeling, bubbling, or flaking on the fascia board
- Dark stains or discoloration
- Visible gaps between the gutter and fascia
- Gutters pulling away or sagging (the fascia can't hold the weight)
- Wasp or bee activity at the roofline (pests exploit soft wood)

**From a ladder (annual inspection):**
- Press the fascia with your thumb - it should be solid. Any softness is rot.
- Look behind the gutter at the fascia surface - dark spots, mold, or crumbling wood
- Check where the fascia meets the soffit - this joint traps moisture and rots first
- Look for carpenter ant frass (sawdust piles) at the base of the fascia

## The Wrong Fix: Painting Over It

Many homeowners (and some contractors) respond to peeling fascia paint by scraping and repainting. This treats the symptom, not the cause. If the wood is still sound, fresh paint buys 3-5 years in Florida before the UV cycle breaks the paint down again and the moisture re-enters.

If the wood is already soft or rotted, painting over it is money wasted - the rot continues underneath the new paint.

## The Right Fix: Aluminum Fascia Wrap

Aluminum fascia wrap is the permanent solution. Custom-bent aluminum covers the entire fascia board, creating a waterproof, UV-proof, maintenance-free surface that:

- **Never rots** - aluminum doesn't absorb moisture
- **Never needs painting** - the color is baked into the finish at the factory
- **Seals against pests** - no soft wood for carpenter ants, termites, or wasps to exploit
- **Supports gutter mounting** - provides a solid, stable surface for gutter hangers
- **Color-matches your gutters** - everything at the roof edge looks like one finished system
- **Lasts 30+ years** with zero maintenance

## Prevention (Before Rot Starts)

If your fascia is still sound:
- **Keep gutters clean and functional** - the #1 prevention is eliminating the water source
- **Maintain paint** - touch up any peeling or bare spots before moisture enters
- **Install gutter guards** - reduces the chance of overflow and backed-up water
- **Annual inspection** - press-test the fascia once a year, especially behind gutters

Fascia rot is a when-not-if problem for wood fascia in Florida. The humidity, rain, and UV exposure guarantee that unprotected wood will eventually fail. Aluminum fascia wrap breaks the cycle permanently - one installation, no maintenance, 30+ year lifespan.
