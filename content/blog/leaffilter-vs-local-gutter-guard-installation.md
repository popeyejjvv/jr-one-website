## What You Get with a Local Specialist

## The Product Is the Same

LeafFilter's micro mesh is a fine mesh over an aluminum support frame. That's the same design every micro mesh guard manufacturer uses. The mesh isn't patented. The frame design isn't unique. The installation method is standard.

What IS unique to LeafFilter is their marketing machine, franchise infrastructure, and in-home sales process.

## Where the Price Difference Goes

LeafFilter's higher price pays for:

1. **National TV and digital advertising** - estimated $100M+/year marketing spend
2. **Franchise fees** - each LeafFilter dealer pays franchise royalties
3. **In-home sales team** - the person who visits your home for 90 minutes isn't an installer, they're a salesperson on commission
4. **The "manager callback" discount** - halfway through the in-home presentation, the salesperson calls their "manager" who authorizes a "today only" discount. This is a scripted sales technique, not a genuine negotiation.
5. **Overhead** - corporate offices, call centers, CRM systems, branded trucks

None of these things improve the product on your gutters.

## Warranty Comparison

LeafFilter offers a warranty, but read the fine print:

- Some warranties are transferable, some aren't
- Coverage may exclude certain damage types
- Claims go through a national corporate process
- Your local LeafFilter dealer may change or close

Local specialist warranties:

- Direct relationship with the company owner
- Issues resolved with a phone call, not a claims process
- The person who installed it is often the person who fixes it
- The company's reputation in the community depends on honoring warranties
