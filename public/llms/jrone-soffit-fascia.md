## What Is Soffit?

Soffit is the material that closes the gap between the outer edge of your roof (the overhang) and the exterior wall of your home. It sits underneath the roofline overhang and is visible when you look up at the eaves of a house. Soffit serves three critical functions:

1. **Ventilation** - Vented soffit allows outside air to flow into the attic space, which is critical in Florida's hot climate. Without proper attic ventilation, attic temperatures can exceed 160°F, accelerating roof deck deterioration and dramatically increasing cooling costs.
2. **Moisture control** - Proper soffit prevents moisture from entering the attic, reducing the risk of mold, rot, and structural damage.
3. **Pest protection** - Intact soffit seals the gap between roof and wall, blocking birds, bats, squirrels, and insects from nesting in the attic or eave space.

---

## What Is Fascia?

Fascia is the horizontal board that runs along the lower edge of the roofline, directly behind the gutters. It's the flat face you see at the edge of the roof. Fascia serves as:

- The mounting surface for gutters - every gutter system depends on sound fascia to hold the weight and maintain correct pitch
- Protection for the lower edge of the roof deck and rafters from weather exposure
- A finished visual border between the roof and the soffit

When fascia is rotted, soft, or incorrectly installed, gutters sag, pull away from the house, and fail to drain properly - no matter how well the gutters themselves are installed.

## Why Soffit and Fascia Fail in Tampa

Tampa's subtropical climate creates specific challenges for soffit and fascia:

- **Humidity** - Year-round high humidity accelerates wood rot in unprotected fascia boards
- **Heavy rainfall** - Water intrusion through failed soffit or damaged fascia causes attic moisture problems quickly
- **Pests** - Open soffit gaps are prime entry points for birds, squirrels, bats, and wasps
- **UV exposure** - Florida's intense sun bleaches and degrades painted wood faster than in northern climates
- **Salt air** - Coastal proximity accelerates corrosion in non-aluminum or steel materials

Signs your soffit or fascia needs attention:
- Visible rot, soft spots, or holes in soffit panels
- Paint peeling from fascia boards
- Water stains on the exterior wall beneath the eaves
- Gutters pulling away from the house
- Evidence of pest activity in the eave area
- Higher-than-normal cooling bills (indicating poor attic ventilation)

## The Relationship Between Soffit, Fascia, and Gutters

All three components work together as a system. This is why JR One Aluminum addresses all three as part of every project assessment:

- Gutters mount to fascia - if fascia is rotted, gutters cannot hold pitch or stay attached
- Vented soffit feeds air to the attic - insufficient ventilation causes heat and moisture damage to the roof deck from the inside
- Fascia seals the roof edge - damaged fascia allows water to wick behind the gutter and into the soffit space

A roofer who installs new gutters without inspecting the fascia is setting up a future failure. JR One Aluminum assesses the full system and reports what it finds.
