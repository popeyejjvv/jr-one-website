## Gutter Questions

**Q: What type of gutters does JR One Aluminum install?**
A: JR One Aluminum installs seamless aluminum gutters custom-fabricated on-site. Available profiles include K-style (5", 6", 7"), half-round (6", 8"), commercial box gutters (7"), and commercial D-style gutters (7"). Copper gutters are also available.

**Q: What is a seamless gutter?**
A: A seamless gutter is fabricated in one continuous piece cut to the exact length of your roofline. It has no joints or seams along the straight runs, only at corners and downspout connections. This dramatically reduces leak points compared to sectional gutters sold in hardware stores.

**Q: Should I get 5-inch or 6-inch gutters?**
A: 6-inch gutters have 40% more water-carrying capacity. For most Tampa homes — given Florida's intense rainfall (46–50 inches annually) and larger roof footprints — 6-inch gutters are often the better choice. JR One Aluminum recommends the correct size based on your specific roof area and pitch during the free assessment.

**Q: How long do aluminum gutters last in Tampa?**
A: Quality aluminum gutters installed correctly last 20–30 years in Tampa's climate. Copper gutters last 50+ years. The main factors that shorten gutter life are improper installation, standing water due to incorrect pitch, and infrequent cleaning leading to weight-induced sagging.

**Q: How often should I clean my gutters in Tampa?**
A: Homes with trees nearby typically need cleaning 2–4 times per year due to live oak leaves, pine needles, and seed pods. Without gutter guards, neglected gutters cause overflow, fascia damage, and foundation issues. Gutter guards reduce cleaning frequency to every 1–3 years for most homes.

## Soffit and Fascia Questions

**Q: What is soffit?**
A: Soffit is the material that covers the underside of the roof overhang — the area you see when you look up at your eaves. Vented soffit allows airflow into the attic, which is critical in Florida's hot climate to control heat and moisture.

**Q: What is fascia?**
A: Fascia is the horizontal board at the lower edge of the roofline, directly behind the gutters. It's what the gutters mount to. Rotted or damaged fascia causes gutters to sag, pull away, and fail to drain properly.

**Q: Why does soffit matter in Florida?**
A: Florida attics without adequate soffit ventilation can reach 160°F+, which accelerates roof deck damage, increases cooling costs dramatically, and creates conditions for mold. Vented soffit is one of the most important components of a Florida home's performance envelope.

**Q: How do I know if my soffit needs replacement?**
A: Signs include visible holes or missing panels, pest activity in the eaves, moisture staining on exterior walls below the roofline, or elevated attic temperatures and cooling bills. JR One Aluminum offers free on-site assessments.

**Q: Can you replace soffit and fascia without replacing the roof?**
A: Yes. Soffit and fascia replacement is completely independent of the roof. It's a common standalone project.

**Q: How long does aluminum soffit and fascia last?**
A: Aluminum soffit and fascia, properly installed, can last 30–40+ years. It does not rot, does not require painting, and is corrosion-resistant — ideal for Florida's humid, salt-air environment.

**Q: What is aluminum fascia wrapping?**
A: Fascia wrapping is covering existing wood fascia boards with custom-bent aluminum coil stock. The aluminum is formed on-site to match the exact trim profile, permanently sealing the wood from moisture and eliminating the need for repainting.
