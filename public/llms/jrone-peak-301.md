## What Is Peak 301?

Peak 301 is a plant-based bio-oil treatment that penetrates asphalt shingles and restores the flexibility lost as shingles age. It's sprayed onto the existing roof, absorbs into the shingle matrix, and recharges the oils that UV exposure and heat cycling have driven out. The result: extended shingle life, restored flexibility, and deferred roof replacement — at a fraction of the cost of reroofing.

---

## Why Peak 301 Matters in Tampa

Asphalt shingle roofs in Tampa live a harder life than roofs anywhere else in the continental U.S.:
- **UV exposure** breaks down shingle oils faster under Florida sun
- **Heat cycling** — attic temperatures over 160°F degrade shingles from below
- **Humidity and driving rain** accelerate granule loss
- **Hurricane wind uplift** stresses aged, brittle shingles to failure
- **Insurance companies** are declining or non-renewing policies on roofs over 10–15 years old

A roof that's lost its flexibility is a roof that's one bad storm away from leaks, claims, and replacement. Peak 301 buys back years of service life — often 5 to 15 additional years per treatment.

## How Peak 301 Works

1. **Pre-inspection** — JR One Aluminum assesses shingle condition to confirm Peak 301 is the right treatment. Shingles that are already failing structurally can't be saved by rejuvenation.
2. **Surface prep** — Roof is cleaned of debris, moss, and loose granules.
3. **Spray application** — Peak 301 is applied uniformly across the shingle surface using professional spray equipment.
4. **Absorption** — The bio-oil penetrates the shingle matrix and chemically re-bonds the asphalt.
5. **Cure** — The treatment cures within hours and is rain-safe same day.
6. **Warranty documentation** — Peak 301 comes with a manufacturer-backed warranty per the manufacturer's terms.

## Who Peak 301 Is Right For

- **Roofs 8–15 years old** with normal wear but no major damage
- **Homeowners deferring replacement** for 5–10+ years
- **Homes with insurance non-renewal pressure** due to roof age
- **Rental property owners** stretching roof life on investment properties
- **Homeowners who want to sell** and need the inspection to read better

---

## Who Peak 301 Is NOT For

- Roofs with existing active leaks, missing shingles, or structural damage
- Roofs past end of useful life (20+ years old and showing it)
- Non-asphalt roof materials (tile, metal, flat roof membranes)
- Homes where insurance has already required replacement
