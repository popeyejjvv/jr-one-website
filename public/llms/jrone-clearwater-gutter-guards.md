## Why Gutter Guards Matter in Clearwater
Clearwater is coastal — which changes everything. Palm fronds, sea grape, and salt-laden debris land in every gutter during storms, and the wet salty debris sitting in the channel accelerates corrosion on non-rated hardware. Standard inland gutter guards with steel or galvanized components can rust through in five years on a Clearwater Beach home. Properly-specified salt-rated micro mesh with stainless or aluminum fasteners solves both problems — keeps debris out, and doesn't itself become a corrosion point.

## FAQ
**Q: My last gutter guards rusted out — what went wrong?**
A: Wrong material for Clearwater's salt environment. Most generic guards use galvanized or plain steel hardware that rusts out within five years within a few miles of the Gulf. Salt-rated micro mesh with stainless or aluminum fasteners is the only spec that holds up.

**Q: Will micro mesh handle palm fronds and sea grape?**
A: Yes. Fronds sit on top and slide off — occasionally a big fresh frond needs a brush after a storm.

**Q: How much for a home on Clearwater Beach or Island Estates?**

**Q: Do gutter guards survive a hurricane?**
A: Properly installed guards ride out storms. Large displaced branches can occasionally shift a section — we inspect for existing customers after named storms.

**Q: Will Belleair's architectural review allow gutter guards?**
A: Usually yes. Belleair has tighter HOA and architectural review rules — we handle the approval process where needed.

**Q: Is copper worth it on a waterfront home?**
A: Yes, if you want permanent. Copper is immune to salt corrosion and pairs with the higher-end Island Estates, Harbor Oaks, and Belleair architecture. Premium price, multi-generational lifespan.

**Q: Do guards work on existing older gutters?**
A: Yes, if the gutters themselves are still sound. We inspect during the quote — if the gutters are failing, we say so before putting guards over a system about to go.
