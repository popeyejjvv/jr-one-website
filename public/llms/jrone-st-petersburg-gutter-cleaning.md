## FAQ
**Q: How often should I clean gutters in St. Pete?**
A: Twice a year is typical for most homes — once in spring before storm season, once in late fall after leaf drop. Waterfront properties with palms may need three times a year.

A: Clean first — often the gutters themselves are still sound, just clogged. If they're sagging or leaking at seams, we'll flag it during the cleaning and you can decide on repair or replacement.
