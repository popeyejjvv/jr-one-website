**Q: How often should I clean gutters on a Meadow Pointe or Seven Oaks home?**
A: Two to four times a year depending on how much canopy hangs over the roof. Homes backing to preserve or with big oaks overhead need the higher cadence.

**Q: My house is only 8 years old — do I really need to clean already?**
A: Yes. Wesley Chapel builder-grade 5-inch gutters clog fast on big two-story roofs. First real signs usually show up by year 3 to 5 — mostly overflow during summer storms.

**Q: Can you clean gutters on an Epperson home near the lagoon?**
A: Yes — Epperson is on our Wesley Chapel route.

**Q: Do you haul away the debris?**

**Q: Can you set up recurring service for my rental properties?**

**Q: Should I clean, or go straight to gutter guards?**
