## Why Gutter Cleaning Matters in Clearwater
Clearwater is coastal — so clogged gutters do more than overflow. Wet salt-laden debris sitting in a gutter channel accelerates corrosion on any non-salt-rated hardware underneath, rotting out the system from the inside. Palm fronds, sea grape leaves, and storm debris collect fast near the Gulf. Countryside and inland Clearwater carry their own oak and pine load. Most Clearwater homes near the water need cleaning three times a year; inland Clearwater twice a year. After any named storm, a priority cleaning should be on the list because hurricane debris packs gutters full in a single event.
