## JR One Aluminum in St. Petersburg

St. Pete has three distinct aluminum-work universes: downtown and historic neighborhoods with 1920s–40s bungalows and Mediterranean Revival homes, waterfront and near-waterfront areas with salt air and hurricane exposure, and the newer developments on the west and south sides with larger roof footprints.
