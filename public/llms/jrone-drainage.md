## Drainage Solutions in Tampa Bay

Gutters move water off the roof. Drainage is what moves it away from the house. In Tampa, where 46 to 50 inches of rain falls per year, much of it in concentrated tropical downpours, the difference between "water ends up at the foundation" and "water ends up 20 feet away where it belongs" is what protects the slab, the crawlspace, the landscape, and eventually the framing.

JR One Aluminum installs underground drainage systems that take the water from the downspouts and route it safely off the property via Schedule 40 PVC, catch basins, surface grates, and pop-up emitters.

## Common Drainage Problems in Tampa Homes

- **Water pooling against the foundation** after every storm
- **Sinking sod, mulch washout, and erosion** near downspouts
- **Standing water in yards** that takes days to drain
- **Crawlspace moisture, mold, wood rot** from persistent ground saturation
- **Driveway or sidewalk ponding** creating hazards
- **Pool cage water intrusion** from bad grading

Most of these issues come back to the same root cause: downspouts dumping straight onto soil right next to the house, with no grading or subsurface drainage carrying the water away.

## Drainage Products and Services

### Schedule 40 PVC Underground Drainage
Heavy-duty PVC pipe buried to carry downspout water to a safe drainage exit point (pop-up emitter, street, swale, or storm drain connection). Rigid, smooth-walled, load-bearing, suited for driveway crossings and long runs. Longevity measured in decades.

### Catch Basins
In-ground boxes with grates that collect surface water from low spots, pool deck edges, patios, or driveway runoff. Connected to the main drainage line and discharged safely away from the structure.

### Surface Grates
Inline grates that capture sheet flow across patios, walkways, and pool decks before it can pool. Tied into the same Schedule 40 PVC drainage system.

### Pop-Up Emitters
Spring-loaded emitters that open when water flows through them and close between rain events, preventing pests and debris from entering the drainage line.

### Concrete Splash Guards
Simple surface-level deflection at downspout exit points. Works for minor situations where the water just needs to be redirected a few feet off the foundation.

### Downspout Extensions and Grading Corrections
Adding length to existing downspouts so water exits past the drip line of the foundation. Combined with re-grading at downspout discharge points so water flows away from the slab instead of back toward it.

### Out of Scope
JR One does NOT install french drains, channel drains, dry wells, corrugated drainage pipe, or offer standalone trenching as a service. If your property genuinely needs a french drain or channel drain, we will tell you so during the assessment and refer you to a contractor who specializes in that scope.

## How JR One Aluminum Designs a Drainage System

Every drainage job starts with an on-site assessment:
1. **Identify water sources** — all downspouts, low points, grading problems
2. **Map existing grade** — where does water currently go? Where does it end up standing?
3. **Identify safe discharge points** — swales, streets, storm drains, pop-up emitter locations
4. **Design pipe layout** — shortest-distance gravity flow, minimum bends, correct slope on Schedule 40 PVC
5. **Add catch basins and surface grates** where surface water needs to be captured before it pools
6. **Price the system** transparently with line items so you know what you're paying for

## Drainage + Gutters + Grading — The Full System

A gutter system that dumps onto bad grading is worse than no gutter system at all because it concentrates water at one point instead of letting it spread. JR One Aluminum evaluates:
- Gutter capacity and pitch

- Surface grading visible at downspout exit points

**Q: How much does an underground drainage system cost in Tampa?**
A: Depends heavily on length of run, whether trenching is required across hardscape, and the number of catch basins and surface grates. Contact JR One Aluminum at (844) 444-3114 for a site-specific quote.

**Q: Do you install french drains or channel drains?**
A: No. Our drainage scope is Schedule 40 PVC, catch basins, surface grates, pop-up emitters, splash guards, and downspout extensions. If your property genuinely needs a french drain or channel drain, we will tell you so during the assessment and refer you out.

**Q: Where does the drainage water go?**
A: To a street, swale, storm drain, or safe discharge point via a pop-up emitter. We design the system so gravity carries the water to a daylight exit, not a buried termination.

**Q: Do I need a drainage system if my gutters are working?**
A: Depends on where the downspouts exit. If they exit into a gutter extension that lets water run 10 or more feet away from the foundation onto a graded lawn, maybe not. If they dump straight onto the foundation or onto a pool deck, yes.

**Q: Will drainage work fix my soggy backyard?**
A: Often yes. Catch basins, surface grates, and routed underground PVC drainage can solve persistent standing-water issues. Severe grading problems may need additional landscape work beyond drainage alone.

**Q: Do you repair existing drainage systems?**
A: Yes. Collapsed PVC pipe, crushed downspout connections, failed pop-up emitters, and clogged catch basins are all repairable.
