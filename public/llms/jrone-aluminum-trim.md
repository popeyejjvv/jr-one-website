## Aluminum Trim Wrapping

Aluminum wrapping is the process of covering existing wood trim, fascia, window casings, door frames, columns, and other exterior wood surfaces with custom-bent aluminum coil stock. The result is a permanently sealed, maintenance-free exterior surface that never needs painting.

### Why Tampa Homeowners Wrap Their Trim

In Florida's subtropical climate, unpainted or poorly maintained wood exterior trim faces:
- Year-round high humidity accelerating rot
- UV exposure degrading paint within 2-4 years
- Moisture intrusion from heavy rain and afternoon storms
- Pest damage (termites, wood-boring insects)
- Repeated cycles of expansion and contraction from heat

Aluminum wrapping eliminates all of these problems permanently. The aluminum is custom-bent on-site to match the exact profile of the existing trim and is available in colors that match or complement the home's exterior.

### Trim Wrapping Applications
- Fascia boards
- Window casings and surrounds
- Door frames and headers
- Columns and posts
- Soffits
- Rake boards
- Any exterior wood surface

## Downspout Installation and Services

Downspouts are the vertical pipes that carry water from gutters down to ground level and direct it away from the foundation. Incorrectly placed or undersized downspouts cause:
- Foundation erosion and potential flooding
- Ponding water against the house
- Landscape damage from concentrated water flow
- Basement or crawlspace moisture problems

JR One Aluminum installs:
- 4-inch round aluminum downspouts
- 3x4 and 4x5 rectangular aluminum downspouts and elbows
- Smooth 3x4 downspouts and elbows
- 4-inch round and 3x4 copper downspouts and elbows
- Adapters and reducers for existing system integration
- Downspout repair and clean-out services

## Drainage Solutions

When downspouts discharge water close to the foundation or in areas where surface drainage is a problem, underground drainage systems move water farther from the structure. JR One Aluminum installs:

- Schedule 40 PVC underground drainage
- Catch basins
- Surface grates
- Pop-up emitters
- Concrete splash guards
- Downspout extensions and grading corrections
- NOTE: JR One does NOT install french drains, channel drains, dry wells, or corrugated drainage pipe, and does not offer standalone trenching service

## Specialty Aluminum Products

### Rain Chains
Rain chains replace traditional downspouts with a decorative chain or cup system that water cascades down visibly. Popular for Asian-inspired and contemporary architectural styles.

### Conductor Heads
Conductor heads (also called leader heads or collector boxes) are decorative transition pieces between the gutter outlet and the downspout. They add an architectural accent and help collect and direct high-volume water flow. Available in standard aluminum and premium copper.

### Rain Barrels
Rain barrels collect rainwater from downspouts for use in landscape irrigation, reducing water bills and managing stormwater runoff.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is aluminum trim wrapping?**
A: It's the process of permanently covering exterior wood trim with custom-bent aluminum. The aluminum is formed on-site to match the existing trim profile and sealed to prevent any moisture penetration. Eliminates the need to repaint and protects the wood underneath indefinitely.

**Q: How do I know if my downspouts are the right size?**
A: JR One Aluminum assesses downspout sizing based on the gutter capacity and the roof's drainage area. A common problem is undersized or too-few downspouts that can't handle Tampa's intense rainfall rates, causing gutters to overflow even when clean and unobstructed.

**Q: Can you install underground drainage to take water away from the house?**
A: Yes. JR One Aluminum installs Schedule 40 PVC underground drainage, catch basins, surface grates, and pop-up emitters to move water away from foundations. We do not install french drains, channel drains, dry wells, or corrugated drainage pipe.
