## Why Gutter Cleaning Matters in Tampa
Tampa averages about 50 inches of rain per year, most of it falling in short intense afternoon storms from June through September. Live oaks shed leaves twelve months a year - not just in fall - and pine needles, Spanish moss, palm fronds, and magnolia seed pods pack gutters tight. Clogged gutters in Tampa overflow within minutes of a storm starting, push water behind the fascia, rot soffit, and saturate the soil at the foundation. In heavily canopied Tampa neighborhoods, gutters typically need cleaning two to four times per year.

## FAQ
**Q: How often should I clean gutters on a Tampa home with oak trees?**
A: Three to four times a year is normal for Tampa properties with live oaks or pines overhead. Homes in open lots can stretch to once or twice a year. Guards drop the cleaning cadence to every two to three years.

**Q: Do you haul away all the debris?**
A: Yes. Every leaf, needle, and seed pod leaves with us - bagged and removed from the property. You don't deal with piles in your yard after we're gone.

**Q: Can you clean gutters on a two-story South Tampa or New Tampa home?**
A: Yes. JR One crews carry extension ladders and safety equipment for two and three-story residential work. We do not ask homeowners to move ladders or spot us.

**Q: What's the difference between gutter cleaning and a gutter tune-up?**
A: Cleaning removes debris. A tune-up also tightens hangers, re-seals miters, and flags failing fascia or soffit before it turns into a bigger repair. Most Tampa homes benefit from the tune-up version at least once a year.

**Q: My gutters overflow in the middle of a run - is that a cleaning issue?**
A: Sometimes. A clog anywhere in the gutter or downspout can cause it. If overflow continues after cleaning, the real problem is usually pitch, undersized 5-inch gutters on a big roof, or a sagging hanger.

**Q: Can you clean and install gutter guards the same day?**
A: Yes. The Deluxe Guard Package bundles cleaning plus guard install so the guards go over a clean system, not a channel packed with oak leaves.

**Q: Do you service commercial properties in Westshore or Downtown?**
A: Yes - strip centers, multi-family, office, and mixed-use. Quoted by linear footage and access.
