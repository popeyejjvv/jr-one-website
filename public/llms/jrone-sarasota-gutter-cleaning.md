## Why Gutter Cleaning Matters in Sarasota
Sarasota County sees coastal storms come in directly off the Gulf - different exposure than inland Tampa Bay. When a tropical system pushes through, palm fronds, sea grape, and salt-laden debris pack into gutters fast on Siesta, Lido, and Longboat. Inland Lakewood Ranch and Palmer Ranch carry oak, pine, and laurel oak loads more similar to Tampa. Either way, wet debris sitting in a Sarasota gutter accelerates hardware corrosion on anything not salt-rated, and overflow during heavy rain can find its way into stucco walls and crawl spaces. Most Sarasota homes need cleaning two to three times a year; waterfront three times minimum.
