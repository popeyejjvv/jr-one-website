## Why Soffit and Fascia Matter in Tampa
Soffit and fascia are the wood and metal behind the gutter and under the roof overhang. In Tampa's climate - about 50 inches of rain a year, high humidity, UV, and salt air near the bay - raw wood soffit and fascia have a hard life. Water from overflowing gutters rots the fascia board, then the soffit panels below. Once rot starts, it spreads along the entire run. Proper aluminum or PVC soffit and fascia (or an aluminum wrap over sound wood) stops the cycle - no more painting, no more rot, and properly-vented soffit improves attic airflow and lowers cooling bills.

## FAQ
**Q: How do I know if my fascia is rotted?**
A: Dark streaks or staining on the wood, paint peeling or bubbling, soft spots when you press with a screwdriver from a ladder, or gutters pulling away because the fascia board can't hold the hangers anymore.

**Q: Can you just wrap the fascia in aluminum without replacing rotted wood?**
A: No. Wrapping over rotted wood traps moisture and accelerates the rot. JR One replaces any rotted sections before the aluminum wrap goes on.

**Q: What's the difference between aluminum, vinyl, and Sagiper soffit?**
A: Aluminum is the durable middle-of-market standard - won't rot, holds paint decades, handles Tampa humidity. Vinyl is cheaper and fine for most homes but doesn't hold up as long. Sagiper is premium PVC with wood-look profiles for high-end architectural homes.

**Q: Do you do the venting for attic airflow?**
A: Yes. Perforated aluminum soffit provides the intake airflow modern Tampa attics need, especially on homes with ridge vents above. Proper soffit venting lowers attic temperatures and extends roof life.

**Q: Squirrels got into my attic through the soffit. Can you fix that?**
A: Yes. Common Tampa problem in mature-tree neighborhoods. We seal entry points and install solid or perforated aluminum that pests can't chew through.

**Q: Can you handle a three-tier decorative fascia on a high-end South Tampa home?**
A: Yes. Two-tier and three-tier fascia profiles are regular work in Hyde Park, Bayshore, and Davis Islands.

**Q: Will aluminum fascia match my paint color?**
A: Almost always. Aluminum coil comes in most common trim colors, and close matches are usually available. We confirm the color before ordering.
