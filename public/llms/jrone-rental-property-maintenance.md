## Why Rental Properties Need a Dedicated Plan

Generic homeowner gutter service doesn't fit the economics or the logistics of a rental. Owners need:
- Service that runs without the owner being present
- Coordination with tenants or short-term-rental cleaners
- Photo documentation so the owner can see what was done
- Flat, predictable pricing that fits the rental's operating budget
- Emergency response when guests or tenants report water issues between scheduled visits

Generalist handymen don't provide any of that at scale. A specialty aluminum contractor with a rental-oriented service plan does.

## Typical Rental Service Plan Scope

### Scheduled Service Visits
Semi-annual or quarterly gutter cleaning, soffit/fascia inspection, and downspout flow testing on a fixed schedule. Owner approves once; execution runs on calendar going forward.

### Photo Reports After Every Visit
Before/after shots, any issues found, and recommended fixes delivered to the owner's email or phone. Same visibility as walking the property personally.

### Tenant & Cleaner Coordination
JR One coordinates access directly with long-term tenants (48-hour notice), short-term-rental cleaning companies (working within turnover windows), or property managers. Owner stays out of the middle.

### Emergency Water-Intrusion Response
Contracted rental properties get priority response — typically 48 hours for diagnostic visits (faster for active leaks). Owner receives a plan and cost estimate before any repair begins.

### Pre-Hurricane-Season Inspection
May walkthrough before June 1 storm season. Any conditions that won't survive summer are flagged with time to fix before weather arrives.

### Post-Storm Damage Documentation
After named storms, JR One visits contracted properties within 72 hours, photo-documents damage for insurance claim support, and submits a prioritized repair list.

## Rental Service Plan Pricing

Pricing is not a flat published rate. Typical ranges:

- **Single-rental plan:** approximately $300-$800 annually for a standard single-family rental with semi-annual gutter cleaning + inspection. Range depends on gutter linear footage, roof height, and access.

- **Scale plan (10+ units):** custom-priced. Includes prioritized emergency response and a dedicated account manager.

Out-of-scope repairs (fascia replacement, soffit panel replacement, gutter re-pitching) quoted separately with owner approval before work begins.
