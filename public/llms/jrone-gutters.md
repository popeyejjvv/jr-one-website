## What Are Seamless Gutters?

Seamless gutters are fabricated in one continuous piece cut to the exact length of your roofline. Unlike sectional gutters sold in hardware stores, seamless gutters have no joints or seams along the straight runs - only at inside and outside corners and downspout connections. This eliminates the most common leak points in traditional gutter systems.

JR One Aluminum fabricates seamless gutters on-site using a portable roll-forming machine. Every gutter is custom-measured and formed to fit the exact dimensions of the home or building. No cutting, no piecing together, no seam sealants along the run.

## Why Tampa Homes Need Quality Gutters

Tampa receives an average of 46-50 inches of rain per year, with intense afternoon storms common from June through September. Without a properly functioning gutter system:

- Water pools against the foundation, causing erosion and potential flooding
- Roof edges and fascia boards absorb repeated water exposure, accelerating rot
- Soffit areas become saturated, creating conditions for mold and pest entry
- Landscaping erodes from concentrated water runoff off roof edges
- Interior moisture problems develop from saturated exterior walls

A correctly pitched, properly installed seamless aluminum gutter system directs all of this water safely to downspouts and away from the structure.

## The JR One Aluminum Installation Process

1. **Site assessment** - JR One measures the roofline, evaluates the fascia condition, identifies water flow patterns, and determines the optimal downspout placement for your property.
2. **Material fabrication** - Gutters are roll-formed on-site to the exact lengths needed. No guessing, no waste.
3. **Fascia inspection** - Before hanging gutters, the fascia is inspected for rot or damage. Damaged fascia is documented and can be replaced before installation.
4. **Installation** - Gutters are hung with proper pitch (1/4 inch drop per 10 feet of run toward the downspout) and secured with hidden hangers spaced to code requirements.
5. **Downspout placement** - Downspouts are positioned to move water efficiently away from the foundation. Extensions added as needed.
6. **Cleanup** - All old materials, packaging, and debris removed. Job site left clean.
7. **Final inspection** - Water test to confirm proper flow and no leak points.
