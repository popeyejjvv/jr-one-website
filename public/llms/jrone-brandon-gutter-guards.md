## FAQ
**Q: I've got pines over my Bloomingdale house — will guards actually work?**
A: Yes, but only with micro mesh. Surface-tension guards and coarse screens let pine needles slide through and still clog. Micro mesh blocks needles and seed pods while still passing water at full volume.

**Q: How much will guards cost for my Brandon home?**
A: Most single-family homes run $1,200 to $2,400 installed. Exact quote depends on linear footage and guard selection. Call (844) 444-3114 for a free measurement.

**Q: Will I still need to clean my gutters after installing guards?**
A: Not multiple times a year. Expect to clean or inspect every two to three years instead of two to four times a year — plus quick debris sweeps off the top of the guard after major storms.

**Q: Do you install over my existing gutters?**
A: Yes. We clean first, inspect the gutters and fascia, and then install. If the gutters themselves are failing we say so before putting money into guards.

**Q: Is JR One cheaper than LeafFilter for Brandon?**
A: Typically yes — 30 to 50 percent less for comparable micro mesh. The LeafFilter premium is advertising and franchise overhead, not better material.

**Q: Do guards hurt my roof?**
A: Properly installed guards sit in the gutter or under the first row of shingles — they do not lift shingles or void a roof warranty when installed correctly.

**Q: Do you work on conservation-backed homes?**
A: Yes — and guards are especially worth the money for them because tree debris is constant.
