## Why Copper Gutters Matter in Tampa
Copper is the premium long-life gutter material. In Tampa's climate - 51 inches of rain, high humidity, UV, and salt air near the bay - copper outperforms painted aluminum on both durability and aesthetic. Properly installed copper gutters routinely last 50 years or more. Over the first three to five years they shift from bright copper through brown to the distinctive blue-green Florida patina that matches the architecture of Hyde Park bungalows, Seminole Heights craftsman homes, and Mediterranean Revival builds across Bayshore.

## FAQ
**Q: Why pick copper over aluminum in Tampa?**
A: Lifespan and look. A good aluminum system is 20-30 years in Tampa. Copper lasts 50 years or more and develops the blue-green patina that matches historic Tampa architecture. On the right house it is a permanent upgrade.

**Q: How much more does copper cost than aluminum in Tampa?**
A: Budget for about 4 to 5 times the aluminum per-foot rate. On a typical Tampa home that is several thousand dollars more, but the system outlasts multiple aluminum replacements.

**Q: How does copper age in Tampa's climate?**
A: Shiny copper for a few months, reddish-brown for a year or two, chocolate brown for the next few years, then the blue-green patina. Tampa's humidity and salt air speed up the transition compared to drier climates.

**Q: Do you work on historic districts like Hyde Park and Seminole Heights?**
A: Yes. Historic-district copper - half-round profile, soldered miters, architectural-review documentation - is regular work for JR One.

**Q: Do you solder the corners or use sealant?**
A: Copper corners are soldered. Sealant on a copper gutter is not a copper install - it's an aluminum install with copper-colored metal. We solder.

**Q: Is copper a smart investment for a Bayshore or Davis Islands home?**
A: Yes. Salt air is the single biggest enemy of painted-steel and low-grade aluminum. Copper is immune to salt corrosion and pairs with the higher-end architecture common in those neighborhoods.

**Q: Can you match existing copper on an addition?**
A: Yes. We can fabricate to match existing profiles and, where possible, accelerate the patina to blend with the original.
